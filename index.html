📌 Quick rules

• XP is earned by completing quests or focus sessions.
• Rewards cost XP and can reduce your level if you spend below a level threshold.
• Levels use increasing milestones: 500 → 1,000 → 2,000 → 4,000…

• Random motivational notifications work best while UttamOS is open.
